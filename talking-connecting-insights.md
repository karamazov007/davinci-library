## 2026-08-20 — Open step / situational vector deep-dive

### From Satyam

- Sharpened the shape toolkit while drilling: "prediction/gripe" and
  "reframe/exaggeration" are really the plain-floor versions of **Irony**
  and **Hyperbole** — borrowed the full mapping from the Humor Development
  taxonomy (`humor.html`) and added **Analogy** and **Misplaced Focus** as
  two genuinely new, usable shapes for openers. [Open step; situational
  vector; shape toolkit]
- Correctly identified that the noticing framework underneath all of this
  matters more than the shapes themselves — without a specific, concrete
  observation, none of the four shapes have material to work with. [Open
  step; presence/attention layer, same root skill as active listening]
- Flagged that Wordplay/puns are especially fragile across a language gap
  (drills happen in English, real delivery will be in Hindi) — a concrete,
  personal instance of the "Wordplay is context-dependent" exclusion.
  [Open step; shape toolkit — audience-fit risk]

### From coaching (Claude-flagged)

- Refined the noticing framework into a clean 1:1 mapping, evidenced
  against real reps from the session: **mismatch → Irony**, **magnitude →
  Hyperbole**, **shape of motion/arrangement → Analogy**, **peripheral
  detail → Misplaced Focus**. The category of what you notice effectively
  picks the shape for you. [Open step; noticing framework]
- Situational vs. task-based-ask blend tell: if there's a real thing you
  need from the other person, the line naturally blends situational
  flavor with a task-based ask (coffee shop, only chair left); if not,
  stay pure situational (elevator, moving day). [Open step; vector
  boundary]

## Patterns across sessions
*(recurring wins / recurring slips, promoted here once they show up 2+ times)*

- **Nailing:** Irony (sharpest, most consistent — spotted subtle
  expectation/reality mismatches unprompted across many scenes). Analogy
  close second, especially on rhythm/motion cues (a duel, musical chairs,
  a "war," a "second movie"). Also, unprompted formation-reads alongside
  drilling itself (checked hierarchy before joking at the printer; flagged
  that a silent packed elevator might not want a line at all) — the
  Approach-step read carrying over into Open-step practice on its own.
- **Still slipping:** Misplaced Focus — repeatedly drifted back to
  commenting on the actual problem instead of fully abandoning it for an
  unrelated detail; needed a few corrections before it landed cleanly.
  Also: under ambiguity, generating 3–4 candidate lines in parallel out
  loud instead of committing to the first workable one (microwave scene) —
  fine in drilling, a real problem live. And: sometimes settling for the
  flat, un-flavored floor on the first pass rather than automatically
  running the decision order (parking lot, airport security) — the habit
  isn't fully automatic yet.
