🎯 Action items — the leverage points, placed on the map

Each hits a named bottleneck or loop. Ranked roughly by leverage (Meadows 1999): information & loop-strength beat parameters.
  1. Make the error visible — video, a metric, a coach's eye. You can't correct what you can't perceive; this is the biggest single jump. Acts on: the feedback delay + the error signal into B1.
  2. One cue per rep, external focus — spend the scarce channel on the single most informative error. Acts on: the working-memory bottleneck & the strength of the error-correction loop B1.
  3. Space it and sleep on it — distribute reps; let consolidation run. Acts on: the consolidation delay — the biological rate limit you can't cram past.
  4. Stop before fatigue — quit while the reps are still clean. Acts on: the fatigue balancing loop B2 — tired reps groove errors.
  5. Do > know — get to contact early — cap theory; theory only earns its keep once it meets reps. Acts on: the paradigm — starves the vicious "theory trap" loop R3.
