- ● **D1·4** — Dive / entry — **when + feet-first + head-first all done** ✓ *(D23–D27)*. Cue: *"Feet-first into deep water: breath in, feet in, then trickle out the nose — hands free to scoop and kick."* **Shallow (or suspected-shallow):** no downward momentum — sit-slide/step in (don't jump), vertical, knees bent to land soft, **arms out & pressing down immediately** to brake.
