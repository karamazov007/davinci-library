## The tree

### D1 — Why masturbation exists (evolution + hormones)
- ● **D1·1** — Proxy, not goal: evolution can't install a desire for reproduction, only for proximate rewards (arousal/orgasm) that *correlated* with it. → opened D2.
- ● **D1·2** — So masturbation = the reward pathway accessed directly, minus the ultimate function. Analogy: candy is to nutrition as masturbation is to mating. Near-universal across species.
- ● **D1·3** — Your risk/reward intuition, sharpened: not a computed ratio — the reward machinery just over-selects the high-reward / low-effort / reliable / available option, which masturbation maxes.
- ○ **D1·4** — Its *own* evolutionary function? Byproduct/spandrel view vs adaptive hypotheses (sperm-competition "cleaning," arousal maintenance, mate-selection roles). Flagged debated; not explored.
- ◐ **D1·5** — The hormonal cascade: testosterone (drive) → dopamine (pursuit/wanting) → orgasm (dopamine spike + opioids + oxytocin) → resolution (prolactin↑, dopamine↓ = refractory + deflation).
  - ○ **D1·5a** — Deeper: post-orgasm neurochemistry & the "chaser / regret" state.
- ● **D1·6** — Tips into compulsion when the *cue migrates* from genuine arousal → internal emotional states (boredom/stress/sleep) → becomes emotional-regulation tool → recruits negative reinforcement.
- ○ **D1·7** — Natural-vs-compulsive line (desire-driven & self-limiting vs state-triggered, escalating, regret-leaving). To be distilled into a cue.

### D2 — Is everything just proxies? (proxy theory of everything)
- ● **D2·1** — Core affirmed: proximate proxies for the ultimate (invisible) goal of fitness. Endorsed your taste / bonding / caregiving (baby-schema) examples.
- ● **D2·2** — Correction 1: dopamine ≠ feel-good. It's *wanting/learning*, not pleasure. An orchestra, not one instrument (opioids=liking, serotonin=contentment, oxytocin/vasopressin=bonding). → opened D3.
- ○ **D2·3** — Correction 2: half of all proxies are *suffering* (pain/fear/disgust/loneliness). Two-valence architecture: approach AND avoid. Most compulsions run on the avoid side. Not yet explored.
- ● **D2·4** — Refinement: not short- vs long-term; the organism *never* sees the ultimate goal, so proxies are used across the board. Real axis = how easily a proxy can be triggered without its function.
- ◐ **D2·5** — Synthesis: every addiction = a *decoupled proxy* (junk food, porn, social media, gambling). This is Tab ②'s mismatch, generalized.
  - ○ **BUILD** — make this a "Proxy Theory of Everything" tab. *(Note: Tab ③ "Porn · Triggers" already built — see T3.)*
- ○ **D2·6** — Caveat: mechanism ≠ destiny. Override layer — contraception, art, adoption, celibacy = deliberately severed/redirected proxies. Experienced meaning stays real. Not yet explored.

### D3 — Liking mechanism + dukkha + the escalation law
- ● **D3·1** — Master sentence: wanting = a promise, liking = the payment; evolution built the promise large & robust, the payment small, fragile & fading.
- ● **D3·2** — What liking *is*: tiny hedonic hotspots (NAcc shell, ventral pallidum, brainstem, OFC), driven by opioids & endocannabinoids. Small & fragile vs wanting's large & robust. Liking is transient by design.
- ◐ **D3·3** — Dukkha linkage: craving is structurally unsatisfiable (a pursuit signal, not a satiation signal); feeding trains it. Maps to unsatisfactoriness + anicca of pleasant vedanā + the hedonic treadmill. Convergence, not proof.
  - ○ **D3·3a** — How equanimity actually operates on the gap.
- ● **D3·4** — The hatred-yet-craving extreme: liking goes *negative* while wanting runs full → proves craving has detached from the object's real value; a blind conditioned pull.
- ● **D3·5** — Escalation law: the reward system is a *difference-detector, not a level-detector*. Constants habituate → you must keep generating prediction-error (intensity OR lateral novelty). Extremity climbs when lateral novelty is exhausted.
- ● **D3·6** — Two escalation engines: pharmacological tolerance (substances, receptor downregulation) vs informational habituation (phone/porn — wanting/novelty-driven, no receptor "substance").
- ● **D3·7** — Takeaway cue: **"Escalation is the receipt that proves the account is empty."**
- ○ **D3·8** — Why the promise systematically *oversells* (prediction-error hype), and how to hear it as hype in real time.
