The Consistency System

Consistency is not a willpower quantity — it is the behaviour of a system of stocks that fill and drain through flows, wired together by feedback loops that either compound your effort or quietly cancel it. Here is the whole machine. The leverage points are left for you to find.

How to read this map

StockAn accumulation — a level that builds up or drains down over time (e.g. Habit Strength). Think of a bathtub.
FlowA rate that fills or empties a stock (the taps and drains). You can only change a stock by changing its flows.
Reinforcing loop (R)A snowball. It amplifies change in one direction — virtuous (growth) or vicious (collapse).
Balancing loop (B)A thermostat. It resists change and pulls the system back toward a set point. The source of most "stuckness."
Arrow polarity — + same direction: cause up → effect up (or down → down) − opposite direction: cause up → effect down A loop is reinforcing if it has an even number of −'s, balancing if odd.

Reinforcing Loops The snowballs

Four of these can work for you (compounding consistency) and two work against you (compounding collapse). The same loop structure runs in both directions — which way it spins depends on where you start it.

Balancing Loops The thermostats that fight you

These are goal-seeking. They are why raw effort erodes and why the system keeps dragging you back to baseline. They are not the enemy — they are constraints. Most durable interventions work by re-setting a balancing loop's target rather than muscling against it.

🎯 Your turn — the leverage points

A leverage point is a place in the system where a small shift produces a large, lasting change in behaviour. Ranked roughly from weakest to strongest, they tend to be: numbers/parameters → buffer & stock sizes → flow & delay structure → feedback-loop strength → information flows → rules → goals → the paradigm (identity) the system serves.
