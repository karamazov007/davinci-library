# Going Under — Down · Bottom · Up

*Blueprint for the L6 build (from discussion D1·3). The going-under sequence for calm, low-fatigue
exploring of a lake/river bottom. Breath-hold first principles live in `staying-calm-airway.md` and D1·3a.*

**Runs through all three phases:** stay **calm and relaxed** (saves oxygen), keep the **arms mostly
streamlined** (they are *not* the engine), **manage the nose** (leak air out to keep water out / pinch
to equalize the ears), and obey the **breath-hold rule** — surface at the first real urge, never alone,
never hyperventilate.

---

## Phase 1 — Going DOWN (the duck dive)

0. Relax on your stomach at the surface (a snorkel lets you relax without lifting the head); take **one full, relaxed breath** (one — not a series of forced breaths).
1. **Pre-equalize:** pinch the nose and gently blow → pop the ears *before* descending, so the first 2–3 m don't hurt.
2. **Fold:** rotate head/chest down — either a *pike* (straight legs, hinge at the hips → "V" then straighten) or a *tuck* (knees to chest → then extend); both are fine.
3. **Legs up & out:** get the legs straight **up and out of the water**, body in one line. Out of the water they're no longer held by buoyancy, so **their weight drops you under — you don't muscle down.** (If you're forcing it, the legs aren't high/vertical enough.)
4. **One long pull:** a single breaststroke pull sweeping all the way to the thighs. *Quality check:* one pull should sink your legs completely — if it takes more, the legs weren't truly vertical.
5. **Equalize again:** as one hand travels back toward the overhead streamline, use it to pinch the nose and gently blow, then send it overhead.
6. **Continue down:** with fins → kick; without fins → another breaststroke pull.

**Duck-dive cue:** *"Breathe & pop the ears → fold to 90° → legs to the sky → one pull to the thighs → pop again → kick on."*

**Equalize cue:** *"Pinch the nose, mouth shut, gently push air toward your ears till they pop."*
*(Equalizing = pinch nostrils shut, gently push air so it goes up into the middle ear and matches the outside pressure → no eardrum pain. Gentle, early, and often. This is the opposite of the airway leak: leak = nose open, air out; equalize = nose pinched, air up to the ears. They alternate.)*

## Phase 2 — At the BOTTOM (explore)

1. **Engine cycle:** kick → **hover** (streamline glide) → when you slow, kick again. "Kick and hover."
2. Arms **joined and pointed** (streamline) during the hover; a long **pull-to-the-thighs only occasionally**, for a boost.
3. **Slow and relaxed**; **look with your eyes**, don't arch the back. Keep equalizing if you go deeper; keep a nose-leak to keep water out.
4. Mind the breath-hold — at the **first real urge, start heading up** (don't push).

**Bottom cue:** *"Kick and hover, kick and hover — arms streamlined, pull only for a boost."*

## Phase 3 — Coming UP (ascend)

1. You're buoyant — **relax, streamline, one gentle kick, and float up.** No pulling.
2. Start **exhaling just before** you break the surface.
3. Break the surface and **roll to a back float or tread** — breathe and settle (the reset).

**Ascend cue:** *"Relax and float up — exhale as you surface, then roll to your back."*

---

## 🎯 Master cue (whole sequence)

**"Down: duck-dive + pop the ears. Bottom: kick-and-hover. Up: relax and float."**
