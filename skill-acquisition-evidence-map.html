🎯 Action items — leverage points placed on the map

Each hits a named bottleneck or loop. Ranked roughly by leverage (Meadows 1999): information & loop-strength beat raw parameters.
  1. Get a clear error signal — but don't over-feed it — you need to perceive the error (video, a metric, a coach), yet reduced/faded feedback retains better than constant feedback. Acts on: the feedback delay + the B1 loop. [Shadmehr; Winstein & Schmidt]
  2. One cue per rep, external focus — spend the small working-memory channel on the single most informative error, aimed at the effect not your body. Acts on: the WM bottleneck & B1; guards against choking. [Cowan; Wulf; Beilock]
  3. Space it and sleep on it — distribute reps; spacing beats cramming and sleep consolidates offline. Acts on: the consolidation delay. [Cepeda; Walker]
  4. Vary & interleave it — random/varied practice feels worse day-of but retains & transfers better than blocked. Acts on: what actually enters the skill stock. [Shea & Morgan]
  5. Do > know — get to contact early — theory is a separate memory system; past a thin layer it doesn't convert to skill and steals reps. Acts on: the paradigm; starves the theory-trap loop R3. [Anderson; Macnamara]
